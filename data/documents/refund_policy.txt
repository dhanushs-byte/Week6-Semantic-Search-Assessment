REFUND POLICY

Eligibility
A customer may request a refund when a payment was duplicated or a service was not delivered as described. Refund eligibility depends on the purchase terms and applicable law.

How to request
Submit a refund request through customer support. Include the order number, payment date and a short explanation. The billing team reviews the request and sends an email with its decision.

Processing
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Bank processing times may vary.
