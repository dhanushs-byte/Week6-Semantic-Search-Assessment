EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY

Purpose
This policy explains how employees request annual leave and how managers approve leave requests. Employees should plan leave in advance and submit requests through the human resources portal.

Request process
An employee signs in to the HR portal, selects the leave type, enters the start and end dates, and submits the request. The manager reviews team availability and approves or declines the request. The employee receives a notification after the decision.

Responsibilities
Employees must check their available leave balance before submitting a request. Managers should respond promptly and ensure business coverage.

Records
Approved leave is recorded in the employee attendance system. Questions about leave balances can be sent to the HR team.
