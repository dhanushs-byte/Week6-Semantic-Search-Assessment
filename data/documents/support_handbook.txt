CUSTOMER SUPPORT HANDBOOK

Creating a ticket
A support ticket should include a clear subject, a description of the problem, the affected product and any relevant error message. Attach screenshots only when they do not expose passwords or private information.

Priority levels
Critical incidents affect many users or prevent an essential service from operating. High priority issues affect an important workflow. Normal requests are handled in received order.

Response and escalation
The support team acknowledges requests and assigns them to the appropriate specialist. Urgent technical incidents are escalated to the on-call engineer. Customers can check ticket status through the support portal.
