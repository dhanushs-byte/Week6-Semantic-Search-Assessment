CUSTOMER BILLING GUIDE

Monthly invoices
A monthly invoice lists the subscription plan, billing period, applicable taxes and total amount due. Customers can download invoices from the Billing section of their account.

Payment methods
Customers can update an expired card or choose another supported payment method in account settings. A payment receipt is available after a successful transaction.

Billing questions
Contact the billing support team if an invoice contains an unexpected charge. Include the invoice number when requesting help.
